Somethin’ Spice

What started as jamming with friends from the UW Free Jam Club in my junior year became a Chinese fusion and jazz band. Our mission is to share the exciting, complex world of fusion and jazz with UW and the surrounding communities.

Members: Leo Zhang (sax), Jiaming Li and Johnny Hu (guitar, Johnny also on trumpet), Xiaohang Zhou (bass), Aiden Chan (drums), Phoenix Hua (keytar), Lixin Qin (guest on drums). Instagram

Kaede01

My good friend Kensho Gendzwill and I founded Kaede01 in our freshman year during Covid, and it’s been an incredible journey watching it grow into one of the most recognized J-pop/J-rock bands at UW. Members: Luna Osaki (vocals), Mason Palmer and Madison Maryani (guitar), Kensho Gendzwill (bass), Max Frissell (drums). Past members: Lisa Benjamin and Connor Bender (vocals), Austin and Chris Yamashita (guitar), Jae Choi and Brin (drums).

UW, CMU CS2 Esports

I’ve always been a big Counter-Strike player, and was fortunate enough to lead the UW CS2 collegiate team to a West Coast trophy, and now the CMU team. Whether it’s the highs of a close match win or the lows of a disappointing early exit, it’s truly special to grow together with people from all parts of the university in a tight-knit team.

One of my fondest memories was a LAN tournament at Syracuse University. We drove ~7 hours one way to upstate New York in the dead of night in freezing winter, and though we were exhausted, we never stopped talking and sharing our love for the game: how we got into it, our favorite pro players and memories of the event. We. Need. More. LANs in America.

Melody in the Stacks

A series of mini concerts at the Tateuchi East Asia Library, showcasing performances focused on Chinese, Korean and Japanese music. It began with a conversation with my supervisor about how to make the newly renovated library more engaging for students. I put together a recurring event, drawing on 3 years of concert organization, graphic design and marketing. Watch
