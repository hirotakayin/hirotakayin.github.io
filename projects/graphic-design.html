The project

I started at the Tateuchi East Asia Library as a student assistant, helping sort post-war maps of Japan. After a quarter, I took on an entirely graphic design role, and I’ve been creating promotional graphics, collection display pieces and informational posters ever since.

What I did

One piece I’m especially proud of is the pamphlet for the library’s Grand Reopening Ceremony, which helped over 150 attendees find their way around the newly renovated spaces. My graphics for the Special Collections Exhibit are on display in the library lobby, showcasing rare and beautiful illustrations from our China, Japan and Korea studies collections. Together with my coworkers Elene Liu and Junseo Park, I also started the library’s own Instagram page to promote our space and new events.
