Somethin’ Spice

What started as jamming with friends from the UW Free Jam Club in my junior year became a Chinese fusion and jazz band. Our mission is to share the exciting, complex world of fusion and jazz with UW and the surrounding communities. Members: Leo Zhang (sax), Jiaming Li and Johnny Hu (guitar, Johnny also on trumpet), Xiaohang Zhou (bass), Aiden Chan (drums), Phoenix Hua (keytar) and me on keys, with Lixin Qin guesting on drums for HuskyJam 2024.

Kaede01

Kensho Gendzwill and I founded Kaede01 in our freshman year, and it has grown into the most recognized J-pop and J-rock band at UW. We’ve been playing for 4 years across several lineups, and it has been one of the constants of my time in college. Current members: Luna Osaki (vocals), Mason Palmer and Madison Maryani (guitar), Kensho Gendzwill (bass), Max Frissell (drums) and me on keys. Past members: Lisa Benjamin and Connor Bender (vocals), Austin and Chris Yamashita (guitar), Jae Choi and Brin (drums).

Melody in the Stacks

A series of mini concerts at the Tateuchi East Asia Library, showcasing underrepresented cultural performances with an emphasis on Chinese, Korean and Japanese music. It all began with a conversation with my supervisor about how to make the newly renovated library more engaging for students. Drawing on 3 years of concert organization, graphic design and marketing, I put together a recurring event to brighten the library and give new musicians a wonderful place to perform. Watch

  • May 2024: I was honored to host a successful kick-off concert featuring Elene Liu and Stephanie Lam.
  • Nov. 2024: Following that success, I organized a longer second concert with more performers and a wider range of music. With many thanks to my friend Juan Posada, we recorded it professionally with two overhead microphones.
