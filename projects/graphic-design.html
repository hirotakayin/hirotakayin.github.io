Summary

I started as a library assistant sorting post-war maps of Japan, but eventually moved on to designing the library’s promotional graphics, exhibit pieces and posters. My pamphlet for the Grand Reopening Ceremony helped over 150 attendees find their way around the renovated spaces, and my graphics for the Special Collections Exhibit showcase rare illustrations in the library lobby. I also started new community events, such as Melody in the Stacks, creating a more welcoming space for the student community.
