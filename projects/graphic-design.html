The project

I started at the Tateuchi East Asia Library as a student assistant, helping sort post-war maps of Japan. After a quarter, I mentioned my graphic design work for my other clubs, and I’ve been creating promotional graphics, collection display pieces and informational posters for the library ever since.

What I did

One piece I’m especially proud of is the pamphlet for the library’s Grand Reopening Ceremony, which helped over 150 attendees find their way around the newly renovated spaces. My graphics for the Special Collections Exhibit are on display in the library lobby, showcasing rare and beautiful illustrations from our China, Japan and Korea studies collections.

I am very grateful to my supervisor, Hyokyoung Yi. She gave me a level of freedom I never thought was possible, from graphic design, to coming up with new community events like Melody in the Stacks, to diving deeper into how the department worked. I’m truly happy that I was able to leave with a scale-model gift of the library made in LEGO.
