Melody in the Stacks

A series of mini concerts at the Tateuchi East Asia Library, showcasing underrepresented cultural performances with an emphasis on Chinese, Korean and Japanese music.

It began with a conversation with my supervisor about making the newly renovated library more engaging for students. Drawing on 3 years of concert organization, graphic design and marketing, I set up a recurring event that gives new musicians a place to perform.

The kick-off in May 2024 featured Elene Liu and Stephanie Lam. A longer second concert in November 2024 added more performers and a wider range of music, professionally recorded with two overhead microphones thanks to my friend Juan Posada.
